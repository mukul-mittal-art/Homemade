Sunkissed lover Oh! My honey, my ‘athering lover,
my thread in time, my wuthering somer,
My knit of hope in clothes much rougher,
My beam of light in late night supper,
Oh! My honey, my darling divine,
my bouncing bunny, my ever so fine,
my soft loafed bread in bitter famine,
my bloodred sweet in old aged wine,
Oh! My honey, my flower delight,
my wings in wind, my phosphene bright,
my softspoke cat under glistening nights,
my sea of slumber under dim lit lights.
Oh! My honey, my ashbound love,
my sonorous hymn, my white feathered dove,
my dutiful angel of the makers above,
my starry white night whom I endlessly belove’
